How this is calculated: the course is worth 100 points (Syllabus: Career Planning Portfolio 5, YouTube Interview Project 10, Sports Technology Presentation 10, Sports Betting Contest 5, Quizzes 10, Class Participation 20, Exam 1 20, Exam 2 20). Bonus points (asking questions on the slide decks, plus your Coin Exchange total) are extra credit added on top. The Coin Exchange value is live and moves with Bitcoin until your coins are sold. "Current grade" divides the points you have earned, including bonus points, by the points graded so far. "Expected final" adds your bonus to the points you have earned, then projects Exam 2 at your exam average and all other remaining work at your average on non-exam assignments. The green line adds the Social Media (+4) and Soccer League (about +5) bonuses you can still earn. "Average needed" is the percentage you would need on all remaining points to reach each grade. Grades are rounded to the nearest whole number (for example, 89.5% rounds to 90%). Grade scale: A 94+, A− 90–93, B+ 87–89, B 84–86, B− 80–83, C+ 77–79, C 74–76, C− 70–73, D 60–69, F 59 and below.

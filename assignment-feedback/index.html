Career Planning Portfolio 2 parts · 2.5 pts each

Assignment instructions
This assignment will force students to think critically about their careers and develop a plan to achieve their individual goals. The portfolio has two parts, and you submit each one separately below. Upload one file at a time and pick the matching part — the feedback you get back is scored out of 2.5 for that part only, not out of 5. Statement of Five-Year Career Plan (no more than 2 pages) — worth 2.5 points - Discuss your future career plan clearly - Your first year is counted from the year you graduate from Arcadia University (e.g., Class of 2026 → first year in the plan is 2026) - Include a graph or table showing your career plan year by year, with explanation (steps to success) Resume (1 page) — worth 2.5 points - Should be up to date

Sports Technology Presentation 10 pts

Assignment instructions
Each student will give a 5-minute presentation to the class. This is an individual project, and students are expected to present how technology (like AI, virtual reality, augmented reality, metaverse, etc.) will change sports and business of sports in the next 10 years. Your presentation should include: - Title Page - Your Idea - Rationale (e.g., why you think it's a good idea) - Expected outcome/impact (i.e., financial, safety, improvement, etc.) - References (cite sources used) Formatting rules: - No more than 5 slides (not including your title page) - Don't have more than 3 sentences in each slide - Failure to adhere to formatting guidelines results in the minimum points for the assignment. Use references (news or scholarly articles) to support your ideas. Present your own ideas — insight and creativity are the main focus of evaluation.

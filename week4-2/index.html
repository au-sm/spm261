● Broadcast Rights
21 / 37

NBC

NBC is the IOC’s single biggest broadcaster. (Ex: BBC, CBC, Eurosport/Discovery)

  • 2021–32: $7.65 billion, extended to 2036 for another $3 billion
  • 2020 Tokyo: $1.45 billion
  • 2024 Paris: $1.69 billion
  • Roughly more than 50% of the IOC’s worldwide broadcast rights
  • NBC has its own designated zones and adds its own cameras to its coverage (nearly 100) — US-focused coverage, alongside the Olympic Broadcasting Services (OBS)

Category Exclusivity

BMW & Team USA

  • In 2010, BMW signed a 6-year deal with the USOC worth $4 million annually.
  • Gained extensive media coverage.
  • Built a new racing wheelchair for the U.S. Paralympic team in 2016, as well as a bobsled in 2014.

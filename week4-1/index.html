● Marketing Mix

Marketing Mix

Product. Price. Distribution.

  • Sport marketing involves product strategies, pricing decision, and distribution issues.
  • These activities are referred to as the marketing mix.
  • Sport marketing mix is defined as a set of elements that sport organizations use to satisfy consumers’ needs.

● Marketing Mix Cont.
11 / 23

(1) Product Strategies

  • Decisions regarding licensing, merchandising, branding, etc.
  • Change of team logo.
  • Developing services (concessions, parking, stadium seating, etc.)

(2) Place Strategies

  • How to deliver sport to spectators in the most effective and efficient way.
  • Where to build a new stadium, how to distribute tickets, etc.
  • Inventory management, warehousing, wholesaling, and retailing for sporting good stores.

(3) Pricing Strategies

  • Fees for personal seat licenses, pay-per view, price of tickets for sporting events

(4) Promotion Strategies

  • Sponsorship, advertising, sales promotions, personal selling, etc.

● Two Major Thrusts
13 / 23

Two Major Thrusts

Sport marketing has developed two major thrusts:

(1)

Marketing of Sport

The marketing of sport products and services directly to consumers of sport.

(2)

Marketing through Sport

The marketing of other consumer and industrial products or services through the use of sport promotions.

● How Does Sponsorship Work?
20 / 23

Consumer Match

An event or team that a company sponsors shares the same target consumers

Ex) Mercedes-Benz — The US Open

Fans watching the US Open have a relatively high income, so Mercedes-Benz wanted to target those with the high income.

● How Does Sponsorship Work?
21 / 23

Functional Fit

The sponsor’s product is used during the event by participants or the event organizers.

Ex) Wilson — MLB

If Wilson is a sponsor of MLB and their baseball gloves are used by the players, then this is a functional fit.

● How Does Sponsorship Work?
22 / 23

Image Fit

A company is looking for an image transfer from the event or team they are sponsoring.

  • Ex) Subway sandwich – Olympic athletes (e.g., healthy image)
  • Ex) Land Rover – US Ski & Snowboard (e.g., winter, strong SUV on snow image)
  • Ex) Coca-Cola – Olympics
